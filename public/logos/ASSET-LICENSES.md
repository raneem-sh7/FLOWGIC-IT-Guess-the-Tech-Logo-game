Technology logo SVGs in this folder are stored locally so the game does not
request them from a third-party host at runtime.

  `git.svg`, `docker.svg`, `react.svg`, `angular.svg`, `kubernetes.svg`,
  `redis.svg`, and `graphql.svg` are from
  [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org/) and are distributed under
  [CC0 1.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/LICENSE.md).
